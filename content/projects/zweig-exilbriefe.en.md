Cataloguing, digitisation and publication of around 500 letters from the estate of Stefan and Lotte Zweig, given to the University of Salzburg by the heirs in 2024: correspondence between Stefan and Lotte Zweig, with family members and with contemporaries from the years in exile. The letters are integrated with metadata into the platform Stefan Zweig digital, which has been reconstructing Zweig's dispersed estate since 2016.
