Edition of Peter Handke's notebooks as facsimile with transcription, commentary and indexes, edited by Katharina Pektor, Ulrich von Bülow and Bernhard Fetz. Phase 1 (online since June 2024) covers 22 notebooks from 1976 to 1979; phase 2 revises the existing material and edits 26 further notebooks up to February 1986.

**My contribution:** Software development for the edition platform.
