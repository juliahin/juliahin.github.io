The MHDBDB is a research infrastructure for Middle High German language and literature, started in 1992 by Horst P. Pütz (Kiel) and Klaus M. Schmidt (Bowling Green) and hosted at the University of Salzburg since 2002. It holds 667 texts with lemmatisation, part-of-speech tags and links to a concept system. Between 2024 and 2026 the entire data set was migrated from RDF to TEI. The data is openly available on GitHub and Zenodo; the new interface MHDBDB Next runs on the TEI files without a database server, while MHDBDB Graph remains as the RDF access.

**My contribution:** Data migration and curation of the TEI corpus, user acceptance testing for MHDBDB Next, project coordination and grant applications, TEI documentation, XPath and XSLT scripting.
