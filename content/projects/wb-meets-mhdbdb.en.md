Integration of the Wenceslas Bible, a Bohemian prose translation of the Bible (c. 1390), into the MHDBDB. Around 150,000 words from five biblical books were lemmatised and POS-tagged in a three-stage pipeline of automation, LLM assistance and manual review; names were aligned with LOD vocabularies (Wikidata, CIDOC CRM, Iconclass). New lexicon entries cover Bohemian-influenced spellings and Old Czech glosses. The Wenceslas Bible has been searchable in the MHDBDB since May 2026 (siglum WZB) with 95.3 % lemma coverage. The pipeline can be reused to ingest further texts.
