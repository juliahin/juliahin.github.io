Complete digital edition of the Wenceslas Bible, the richly illustrated German Bible translation made c. 1390–1400 for King Wenceslas IV and held by the Austrian National Library: facsimile, transcription, reading text and Vulgate synopsis, plus analysis of the image programme and of text–image relations. The edition is published on the Austrian National Library's edition platform; Genesis to Joshua have been available with transcription, reading text and synopsis since September 2025.

**My contribution:** Image annotation workflows, iconographic and semantic analysis of text–image relations, controlled vocabularies, evaluation of computer-assisted image analysis (object and face detection) and Iconclass classification with a RAG pipeline.
